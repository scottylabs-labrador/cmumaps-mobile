# CMU Maps

The campus map of buildings, rooms, and the routes between them.

## Language

**Building**:
A campus structure with a code, a name, and one or more floors.
_Avoid_: Location, place

**Room**:
A named space on one floor of a building.
_Avoid_: Location, place

**Floor**:
One level of a building.
_Avoid_: Story

**CUC**:
The public building. Its floors can be seen without a signed-in person.
_Avoid_: Public location

**Waypoint**:
The start or end of a route: a person's position, a coordinate, a building, or a room.
_Avoid_: Pin, location

**Fastest**:
The route kind with no extra cost for walking outside.
_Avoid_: Shortest

**Inside**:
The route kind that prefers staying indoors.
_Avoid_: Indoor
